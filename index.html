YOUR SHOT

Draw the flight you want, then release to kick. The ball follows the whole gesture: a straight shot, a sideways bend, an arch that drops into goal, or an S-shaped path. No drawn line covers the pitch.

Mouse, phone or tablet: draw the same gesture with your mouse, finger or pen. Finish at your target inside the goal.

Keyboard: arrows aim, Space shoots. Q / E adjust curl. Esc pauses.

Difficulty: Easy gives you more time and space. Normal adds pressure. Hard has faster reactions. Change it from the menu or before any penalty.
